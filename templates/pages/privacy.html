Privacy Policy

How we securely process, protect, and handle your career profile and resume data.

Zero Data Selling Encrypted Vault Full Data Control

1. Information We Collect

We collect account credentials (email, name), uploaded resume documents and pasted LaTeX markup, target job descriptions, mock interview conversational transcripts, coding submissions, and mentorship booking appointments to deliver our core AI analysis features.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your career data is processed strictly to perform ATS scoring, generate tailored LaTeX resumes, compute keyword match ratios, deliver interview feedback, and schedule 1:1 sessions. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers.

3. Storage & Security

All sensitive passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using industry-standard PBKDF2-SHA256. Database records and uploaded documents are stored in secure environments with role-based access restrictions.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You maintain full ownership of your data. You may delete any saved LaTeX resume from your vault at any time, or request full account deletion through our support team.
